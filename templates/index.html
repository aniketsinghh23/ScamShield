How the Idea Began

Built from a real-world problem.

The idea for ScamShield-AI came from seeing how easily a realistic message can make someone click first and think later. We imagined a friendly digital shield that helps people pause, check, and understand the risk in real time.

Our goal is not only to detect threats, but to turn every scan into a small lesson that helps people feel more confident online.
